# Brag Plan: Pocket Lab

## Planning rubric

1. **App:** a research workspace connecting literature, cited findings, hypotheses, and experiments in a Neo4j evidence graph.
2. **Strongest claim:** “One idea. Every connection.” The viewer can ask the graph and review an AI-proposed experiment.
3. **Visual hook:** one research question surrounded by connected evidence, using the product's cream and sage identity.
4. **Actual product:** a real capture of the running Knowledge graph, plus source-derived, enlarged reconstructions of its question panel and proposal review.
5. **Shortest satisfying film:** 25 seconds, landscape, 1920×1080, 30 fps.
6. **Tone:** polished, a calm scientific product film with purposeful UI interaction.
7. **Audio:** warm, low-volume music with three restrained interaction/reveal sounds. No narration was requested.
8. **Caption:** Pocket Lab connects a research question to its evidence, then helps you decide what to test next. Neo4j for evidence, Nosana for reasoning, Daytona for experiment execution.
9. **Flow:** open an idea, explore its connected evidence, ask a question, review a proposed experiment.

## Angle and hook

“Your next experiment starts with evidence.” The film moves from a question into a real graph, then into an actionable research proposal. It demonstrates the relationship between the tools rather than claiming scientific or financial improvements.

## Visual identity

- Ground: #f7f8f4, panels #f0f3eb, deep green #29372b.
- Ink: #293128, readable sage #526c40, muted ink #657363.
- Nosana accent: #794b9b; Daytona accent: #216d7d.
- Display: Manrope 800 and 700. Body: DM Sans 400, 600, 700. Local font files.
- LOOK: warm research notebook, 104px Manrope headings, 32px DM Sans reading text, 2px sage borders, generous 140px side margins, a genuine graph capture.
- BANNED: invented performance numbers, fake paper titles, secret/configuration screens, stock footage, floating title loops, generic SaaS claims.

## Storyboard

### 1. The question — 0–3.3s

Pocket Lab mark. “Your next experiment / starts with evidence.” One key term turns sage. The title lands fast and holds. A research-question line joins beneath it.
Sequential/interaction: headline then research question.
Audio: a clean opening music bed and one soft landing.
Transition: the question continues into the workspace.

### 2. The evidence graph — 3.3–9.8s

“One idea. / Every connection.” On the left, three short steps arrive: sources, passages, hypotheses. On the right, the actual running Neo4j graph appears inside the product's panel styling. A small live-product label distinguishes this from the next illustrative animation.
Sequential/interaction: the three evidence types appear in order, then a traced connection is emphasized by the surrounding layout.
Audio: music supports the graph reveal, a soft impact on arrival.
Transition: the graph panel moves left while an enlarged question panel replaces it.

### 3. Ask and propose — 9.8–19.0s

“Ask the graph. / Find your next test.” A reconstruction of the real “Ask your knowledge graph” panel shows the question “How does degradation change our strategy?” A cursor selects “AI: propose experiments.” A clearly labeled illustrative proposal appears: “Degradation-aware dispatch”; hypothesis “A wear penalty reduces unnecessary cycling”; baseline “Dispatch without a wear penalty”; metrics “Net return · energy cycled · constraint violations”; evidence references G1 and G2. The review button appears last.
Sequential/interaction: question reveal, click, proposal fields in order.
Audio: one restrained click at the simulated button selection.
Integrity: the answer is illustrative copy, not a recorded live model response, published paper finding, or measured outcome. The scene visibly says “Illustrative proposal”. No automatic execution or saved state is implied.
Transition: the proposal's green panel expands into the closing brand stage.

### 4. The lab — 19.0–25.0s

“Pocket Lab” with “Keep the evidence. Test the idea.” Three concise service roles: Neo4j / Evidence database; Nosana / AI reasoning; Daytona / Experiment sandbox. Final brand holds for over two seconds.
Audio: last warm music phrase, fade over final 1.5 seconds.

## Public-safety and authenticity

The graph capture shows the user's existing public-research workspace, with no browser address bar, configuration, credentials, email addresses, or customer information. The enlarged question/proposal sequence is an illustrative UI reconstruction based on src/graph-chat-ui.js and the actual successful feature test. It contains no invented results. All creation and rendering remain local.

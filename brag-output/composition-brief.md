# Pocket Lab composition brief

Create the 25-second landscape film in brag-plan.md using Brag's workflow and HyperFrames. Output brag.mp4 and a settled poster brag.jpg, with the poster baked into frame zero.

Sources: index.html; src/style.css; src/workspace.css; src/existing-graph.js; src/graph-chat-ui.js; CONCEPT.md; archi.md. Product copy used verbatim: “One idea. Every connection.”, “Ask your knowledge graph”, “AI: propose experiments”, “Review and save experiment”.

LOOK: 1920×1080; cream #f7f8f4, deep green #29372b, ink #293128, sage #526c40; Manrope 800 headlines, DM Sans 400/600 body. Keep title text in the 80% safe area. All assets, fonts, GSAP, and sound are local. The real graph capture is assets/graph-live.png, unaltered. Show it without claiming to display an exhaustive evidence review.

Use one seekable sub-composition per scene and register a paused timeline for each. Motion should carry the viewer from a question into its graph, then into a proposal. Short entrances and long reading holds. The simulation must clearly say “Illustrative proposal”. No live model latency, fabricated measured results, automatic execution, or off-screen data mutation.

Audio: bundled Vol. 12 at 0.30 with a 0.5-second opening fade and a 1.5-second closing fade. Soft reveal accents at 0.1 and 3.3 seconds, one click when the AI proposal button is selected. Audio-reactivity omitted deliberately for restraint. Credit in CREDITS.md and sharing copy.

HyperFrames modules read from its installed npm distribution. The current distribution consolidates the legacy hyperframes-registry skill into CLI catalog commands; transition registry searched before composing. Use simple source-specific motion rather than an unrelated shader effect.

Validation: hyperframes check --json with actual nonzero text contrast coverage and no errors; inspect scene midpoints and decoded MP4 frames. Render locally. Keep the editable composition alongside the video. No external publication.
